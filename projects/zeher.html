Scene I

Setting the Stage

As USC Zeher's Social Media Chair and Production Chair, I led the team's visual content and social-media presence from October 2024 through May 2026. I developed campaign concepts, designed event graphics, filmed and edited short-form video, wrote captions, and created promotional content supporting recruitment, performances, workshops, fundraisers, and sponsored events.

Brand Awareness

Used trend-driven concepts, team personality, and consistent publishing to expand Zeher's reach and recognition.

Recruitment & Community

Created timelines, workshop promotions, practice announcements, and team-culture content that made participation approachable and visible.

Events & Revenue

Designed promotional graphics for social events and sponsored partnerships that helped Zeher receive several thousand dollars, including $100 per sponsored post through BIH Events and Radio5 Events.

Scene II

Choreographing the Feed

I created visual assets for recruitment, practices, performances, fundraisers, team culture, and sponsored social events—adapting Zeher's identity to different audiences while keeping the team recognizable.

Scene III

In Motion

I developed each featured TikTok from start to finish—including the concept, filming, editing, caption writing, and publishing. Most videos were created to build brand awareness, showcase team personality, and keep Zeher visible beyond competitions and performances.
